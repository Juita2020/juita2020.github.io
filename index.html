The Problem

Good products don't always have an obvious route to market.

You may have a strong product and some customers, but still struggle to answer a simple question: where should the next commercial effort go?

01 — THE QUESTION

Who should we actually target?

The ICP may be broad, outdated or based on assumptions rather than evidence.

02 — THE RESEARCH GAP

The team starts with a contact list.

Names are easy to collect. Understanding why an account might care is harder.

03 — THE OUTREACH

Generic messages go out.

Low response creates uncertainty about the market, message or targeting.

04 — THE LEARNING

Useful signals get missed.

Without a structured feedback loop, the next campaign often starts from scratch.

Start with the opportunity, not the contact list.

Who I Help

Best fit for B2B technology companies that need a conversation, not a contact dump.

I work best where the product has a clear business use case and the team is willing to learn from the market.

A good fit when...

  • You sell B2B technology or services.
  • You have a clear use case, or are willing to define one.
  • You need help identifying the right accounts or markets.
  • Your sales team can handle relevant conversations.
  • You want to test a new country or segment.
  • You are exploring partnerships or channel opportunities.
  • You want research and outreach connected, not handled separately.
  • You value quality and learning over raw lead volume.

Probably not a fit when...

  • You want thousands of cheap leads.
  • You expect guaranteed meetings or revenue.
  • You want mass automated outreach with little targeting.
  • Your product or offer has no clear business problem behind it.
  • You aren't prepared to follow up with genuine opportunities.
  • You expect one campaign to prove an entire market.
